Open `citibike_non_hmm_baselines.ipynb`: the finalized **26-cell** notebook, including saved outputs and **six figures**. It compares historical hourly averages, activity-adjusted averages, and a 48-dimensional Gaussian with conditional inference. The underlying models and validated predictions are unchanged.

## Methodology and fixed results

The panel contains **250,508 directional trips** between the Upper East Side (A, residential proxy) and Midtown (B, commercial proxy), over 363 dates in 2019. Training/validation/test contain **242/61/60 days**: January–August / September–October / November–December. Every model predicts hours **12–23** from both directions' observations in hours **0–11**.

| Model | Definition | Test MAE | Test RMSE |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Historical hourly average | Full-training weekday/weekend hourly means | 8.656 | 12.916 |
| Activity-adjusted average | Same shape, scaled by observed/expected joint morning activity | 3.867 | 6.210 |
| Multivariate Gaussian | Separate raw-count weekday/weekend distributions; conditional mean | 4.565 | 6.843 |

The activity ratio is unshrunk, with a scale of one if expected morning activity is zero. The Gaussian retains its original **validation-selected alpha=0.5** and training-variance-based ridge. Selection minimized mean validation RMSE across cutoffs 6/9/12/15/18; it was not chosen on test performance. No train+validation refit occurs. The notebook explains the Gaussian assumptions, regularized covariance, conditional mean/covariance, and zero clipping of continuous forecasts.

Scores pool the **1,440 afternoon test cells** (60 days × 12 hours × 2 directions). This is the original retrospective **finalized departure-count** benchmark: a morning ride's destination can become known after noon. It is not a guaranteed live information set. The notebook explains why proportional activity adjustment transfers better to the quieter test period here, while distinguishing that finding from a causal explanation.

## Exact geography and preprocessing

Use the supplied station registry for matching HMM experiments. Coordinate bounds are **half open** (minimum included, maximum excluded):

| Zone | Latitude | Longitude |
|---|---|---|
| A: Upper East Side | [40.765, 40.790) | [-73.973, -73.945) |
| B: Midtown core | [40.748, 40.762) | [-73.995, -73.967) |

Registry selection used January–August departure dates only, excluding DST dates. Valid rides need both station IDs/timestamps and timestamp-derived duration in **[60, 86,400] seconds**. IDs are strings; normalize legacy `123.0` to `123`. Count each in-rectangle endpoint, including within-zone rides and rides with the other endpoint outside. Require **100 endpoints and 30 active departure dates** per station/zone; assign relocated IDs to their dominant training zone. The registry resolves ties, and no test-period stations are added.

Directional features require each endpoint's coordinates and registry ID to agree with its zone. Combine rider categories, bucket AB/BA trips by New York local departure hour, and retain zero-count hours. Midnight-crossing rides belong to their departure date. Exclude **2019-03-10 and 2019-11-03**; holidays retain their weekday/weekend classification. Candidate duplicates use the stable pandas hash of `(started_at, ended_at, start_id, end_id, bike_id)` across chunks; none were found.

For HMM comparison, use these same data, splits, morning observations and afternoon targets, resetting sequences each day. Columns are all `AB_00`…`AB_23` followed by all `BA_00`…`BA_23`; the sequence view is `(day, hour, direction)`. The teammate notebook's current 2024, flow-defined zones require alignment before comparing scores. No HMM is implemented here, and geographic proxies do not identify individual travel purpose.

Original public source: https://citibikenyc.com/system-data. The exact 2019 source URL/hash is recorded in the included configuration and provenance files.
